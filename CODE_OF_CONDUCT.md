# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, collaborative, harassment-free community. Welcome different experience levels, discuss ideas rather than people, and give specific, constructive technical feedback. Discrimination, intimidation, harassment, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these standards and may warn, restrict, or ban participants when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate and protect the community.

For sensitive reports, contact the maintainer privately using the methods available on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/eChrls). Do not publish sensitive information in an Issue.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
